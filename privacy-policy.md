# Privacy Policy for Braintino

**Last updated: July 4, 2026**

Braintino ("the app", "we", "us") is a casual brain-puzzle game published by
**Techtory OU**. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and
does not handle. Braintino has no user accounts, does not ask for any personal
information, and keeps your game progress on your device. The only information
that leaves your device is a limited amount of **anonymous, aggregated usage
data** used to understand how many people install and play the app.

If you have any questions, contact us at **info@techtory.io**.

---

## Summary

- We do **not** ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email,
  phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.
- We do **not** use accounts, logins, or sign-ups.
- We do **not** show ads, and we do **not** use advertising identifiers (no IDFA,
  no Android Advertising ID).
- We **do** use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to collect anonymous,
  aggregated usage statistics (for example, how many people install, open, and
  return to the app).
- We do **not** sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.
- Your game progress stays **on your device**.

---

## Information We Collect

Braintino does **not** collect personally identifiable information. We do not ask
for or store your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts,
photos, or payment details, and the app does not require you to create an account.

The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous analytics described in the
["Analytics"](#analytics) section below.

## Data Stored on Your Device

To let you keep your progress, Braintino saves the following **locally on your
device only**, using your device's standard app storage:

- Game progress (levels, stars, XP, coins, streaks, and session history)
- Unlocked and selected cosmetic items
- Your settings (sound, haptics, relaxed mode, accessibility preferences)

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. If you
delete the app, this data is removed with it. You can also clear it at any time
from **Settings → Reset progress** inside the app.

## Analytics

Braintino uses **Google Analytics for Firebase**, a service provided by Google
LLC, to understand how the app is used in aggregate so we can improve it. This
helps us answer questions like how many people install the app, how often they
come back, and how many people play each day or month.

**What is collected.** Firebase Analytics automatically collects a limited set of
non-personal, event-based information, including:

- App usage events such as app installs (first open), app opens, session starts,
  screens viewed, and time spent in the app.
- Basic device and technical information such as device model, operating system
  and version, app version, language, and a coarse **region/country** (derived
  from your IP address).
- A randomly generated, resettable **app-instance identifier** that lets Google
  count unique installs. It is not your name and is not linked to your real-world
  identity.

**What is NOT collected.** We do not collect your name, email, phone number,
precise location, or any content you create. We do **not** use advertising
identifiers (IDFA on iOS or the Android Advertising ID) and this data is **not**
used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites.

**How we use it.** We use this data only to measure installs, retention, daily and
monthly active users, and general engagement, and to fix problems and improve the
game. We look at it in aggregate, not to identify individuals.

**IP addresses.** Firebase Analytics uses your IP address to derive an approximate
region and then discards it; we do not receive or store your IP address.

**Google's role.** Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider
and also as described in its own policies. You can learn more here:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Because Google operates globally, this anonymous analytics data may be processed
on servers located in the United States or other countries.

## App Updates (Network Use)

Braintino can receive small over-the-air content and code updates so we can fix
issues and improve the game without requiring a new store download. To check for
and download these updates, the app contacts the **Expo Application Services
(EAS) Update** servers operated by Expo (`u.expo.dev`).

When this happens, standard technical request information — such as your device's
IP address, operating system, and the app's runtime version — is processed by
Expo solely to deliver the correct update. This does **not** include any personal
information or game data. You can learn more in Expo's privacy policy at
https://expo.dev/privacy.

## Advertising

Braintino contains **no advertising** and integrates **no advertising SDKs**. We
do not use advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or the Android Advertising ID)
and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

## Purchases

The current version of Braintino contains **no in-app purchases** and does not
process any payments. All cosmetic items are earned through gameplay using in-game
coins that have no monetary value.

## Children's Privacy

Braintino is a general-audience puzzle game. It collects no personal information
from anyone, including children. The anonymous analytics described above are not
directed at children and are not used to build profiles or to advertise. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the
equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you have concerns, contact us at
info@techtory.io.

## Data Security

Braintino does not store your personal data on our own servers. Game data on your
device is protected by your device's operating-system security, and the anonymous
analytics data is handled by Google's infrastructure under Google's security
practices.

## Your Rights and Choices

Because the analytics we collect are anonymous and not tied to your identity, we
cannot look up, correct, or delete data about a specific person. You still have
full control in the following ways:

- **Your game data:** reset it any time from **Settings → Reset progress**, or
  remove it entirely by uninstalling the app.
- **Analytics:** uninstalling the app stops all further analytics collection. On
  Android you can also reset or limit ad-related identifiers in your device
  settings, though Braintino does not use them.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a region with
similar laws, and you have questions about how this data is handled, contact us at
info@techtory.io.
